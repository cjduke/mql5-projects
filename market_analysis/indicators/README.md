The Gold/Silver Ratio indicator(GoldSilver_Ratio_Indicator) displays the live ratio between gold and silver in a separate subwindow. It plots three lines: the ratio itself (blue), an upper band marking the 80th percentile of the last fifty bars (red dotted), and a lower band marking the 60th percentile (green dotted). The bands are rolling, not static. They move with the market. When the ratio climbs above the red band, silver is historically cheap relative to gold. When it falls back below the green band, the imbalance has corrected.

The Pearson Correlation indicator(GoldSilver_Pearson_Indicator) displays, in its own subwindow, the rolling twenty-bar Pearson correlation of the two metals' daily returns. The level 0.18 is drawn as a horizontal reference line. When the correlation histogram sinks below this line, gold and silver have temporarily decoupled — a condition that typically reflects silver's industrial demand pulling it away from gold's monetary behaviour, and one that historically precedes the strongest mean-reversion moves.

The Master indicator(GoldSilver_Master_Indicator) combines both analytical views into a single chart while retaining their separate scales. When it is attached to a Daily XAGUSD chart, it plots the ratio and its percentile bands in the main subwindow and automatically loads the Pearson indicator into a second subwindow. One click, and the trader sees the entire framework: the ratio, the bands, and the correlation — all updating in real time.
